# 🏥 Healthcare Analytics
Healthcare generates large volumes of patient, treatment, and diagnostic data.
Analyzing this data helps improve patient care, optimize resource allocation, and support informed decision-making.

## 📌 Project Overview
This Healthcare Analytics project uses Excel, MySQL, Power BI, and Tableau to analyze hospital data and visualize key KPIs related to patient care, doctor workload, treatment costs, and revenue performance. The project demonstrates how data-driven insights can support better clinical decisions and operational efficiency in healthcare organizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Excel (data cleaning & analysis)

MySQL (SQL queries & KPIs)

Power BI / Tableau (interactive dashboards)

## 🔍 Key Findings
1. The hospital served 10,000 patients with 1,000 doctors, maintaining a balanced patient-to-doctor ratio.
2. Top diagnosed conditions, follow-up rates, and lab results highlight areas for preventive care and continuous treatment.
3. Treatment costs, revenue, and average visit cost provide insights into financial efficiency.
4. Interactive dashboards enable easy tracking of operational and clinical KPIs.

## 📊 Overall Insights
1. Provides a holistic view of hospital operations, covering patients, doctors, and finance.
2. Identifies areas requiring focused care and preventive interventions.
3. Dashboards support data-driven decisions and optimized resource allocation.
4. Demonstrates how healthcare analytics improves operational efficiency and patient outcomes.

## ✅ Conclusion
This project showcases how healthcare data analytics can enhance decision-making, resource planning, and patient care. By combining Excel, Power BI, Tableau, and SQL-based analysis, the dashboards deliver actionable insights into hospital performance and clinical trends.
